# Galaxy Fire Studios — Phase 3 final update

This build combines the site-wide sales/payment layer with the approved compact Beat Store design.

Beat Store:
- Beat catalogue is now a compact dropdown inside the vinyl player.
- Search and Major/Minor filters live inside the dropdown.
- The separate All Beats table has been removed.
- 15-second previews, 33⅓ RPM vinyl rotation and gradual stop behavior are preserved.
- Audio remains preload=none.

Sales:
- Existing Paystack configuration is preserved.
- Beat, studio/visual booking and equipment-store verification routes are included.
- Central sale email is configured to default to galaxyfirestudios@gmail.com.
- Exclusive beats remain visible and previewable but are blocked from purchase after sale.
